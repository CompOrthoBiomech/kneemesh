Configuration¶

Each pipeline reads one of these models from JSON with from_json(). Unknown keys are rejected. LabelMapBrep and BrepMesh hold the settings every structure shares, and a section for each kind of implemented structure holds its own settings. Keys of labels, label_maps, and breps must be names from Structure.

Bases: BaseConfig

Settings for building a B-rep of every structure in a segmented LabelMap.

The segmentation is either one multilabel LabelMap, label_map, with labels naming its values, or several single-label LabelMaps, label_maps, that are assembled onto one grid. Give one or the other. Each structure named in labels or label_maps is built by its own pipeline, with the settings of its section such as meniscus, and written to <output_directory>/<structure><output>.
