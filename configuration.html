Configuration¶

Each pipeline reads one of these models from JSON with from_json(). Unknown keys are rejected. LabelMapBrep and BrepMesh hold the settings every structure shares, and a section for each kind of implemented structure holds its own settings. Keys of labels, label_maps, and breps must be names from Structure.

Settings for building the B-reps of the lateral and medial tibial and the patellar cartilage, with the defaults of each step.

Each is a sheet over the plane normal to its thinnest direction, between its articular side and the side against its bone, within a smooth perimeter. Defaults left as None depend on hex_blocks. Either way the B-rep is one solid, which hexahedral meshing partitions itself.

Bones¶

The femur, tibia, and patella sections each hold one bone’s settings. A bone’s geometry is its closed, triangulated surface, written as .stl, rather than a B-rep, and it is meshed with its own element_type: triangles or quadrilaterals on its surface, or tetrahedra, never hexahedra.
