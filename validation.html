Validation¶

kneemesh is developed against one knee, the structures in tests/dat. To see how it generalizes, examples/validation/all_oks.py runs the full assembly, B-rep, and mesh pipeline on every structure of the OpenKnee(s) subjects’ segmentations, which examples/validation/crop_oks.py crops to their labels and stores as sitkUInt8, as tests/dat is. The script runs locally, since the data is not distributed with kneemesh, and writes this page.

Generated 2026-11-18 09:50 with kneemesh at commit d6b71754ad5c58cbd431abc7736243bb7a51edcd, shapes hex, tet, 8 jobs, timeout 3600 s per step, 173 s in all.

Settings follow examples/knee_*.json; each subject’s output directory holds the configurations to repeat it with the console scripts. SJ is the scaled Jacobian (1 ideal, <= 0 inverted), measured on corner nodes; raw is before hexahedral optimization. aspect is the Frobenius aspect ratio. warp is the boundary quadrilaterals’ warp in degrees. vol is the mesh’s volume over the assembled label’s voxel volume; bones are cut at their shafts, so theirs is not comparable. Flags are heuristic: inverted elements, minimum SJ below 0.2 (hex) or 0.05 (tet), vol outside 0.85-1.08, or an expected set with no faces.
